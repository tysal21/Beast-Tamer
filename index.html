Cookpot

Recipes use any fish of the listed rarity. The pot holds one meal at a time: cooking a different dish replaces the current one; cooking the same dish again extends it (max 60 min).

Fish Codex

Each species' permanent bonus grows with mastery: first catch gives 25%, Silver 60%, Gold the full bonus, and every further Gold-sized batch adds +10% more (up to Gold +10, double the bonus). Legendary and Mythic fish are extremely rare. The number on each card is how many you have stored out of that species' own space, so a rare catch never pushes anything out.

PRIMAL ASCENSION RITUAL

Sacrifice Your Camp for Bloodstones

Ascending resets your current run's Meat, Generators, Weapons, Training, and untames the 4 Milestone Bosses so you can hunt them anew. You permanently retain all Trophy Relics, Beast Forge access, Hybrids, Bloodstone Perks, and Realm Expansions. Hybrids sleep until both of their parents are re-tamed, and Relics sleep until their boss is re-tamed. Bosses stay as strong as they were, so every Ascension leaves you stronger than before.
